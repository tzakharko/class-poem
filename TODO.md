# Git Practice Exercise

In this exercise, you will complete a series of tasks by editing the file `poem.md`.

**Instructions:**
- Complete each to-do item **one at a time**.
- After completing a to-do item, **submit it as a separate commit**.
- Each commit should include:
  - The required edit to `poem.md`
  - Marking the corresponding checkbox as completed in this `TODO.md`
- **Write a meaningful commit message** that briefly describes the change (e.g., "Added missing line to stanza 1", "Fixed rhyme in second stanza").

**Structure of `poem.md`:**
- Title at the top
- The body of the poem
- Your name at the bottom
